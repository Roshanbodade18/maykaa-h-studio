About the label

मायkaa celebrates heritage with modern elegance.

Created by H Studio, the label brings a National Institute of Fashion Technology design eye to authentic Indian sarees and kurtas. Every collection balances traditional textile identity with clean styling, premium presentation, and a confident contemporary mood.

Think Kanchipuram silk for ceremony, Paithani weaves for heirloom color, Banarasi silk for luminous detail, and designer kurtas for refined everyday occasions.
